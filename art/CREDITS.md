# Artwork

The paintings themselves are all centuries old and in the public domain. Files were taken from
Wikimedia Commons; where the uploader attached a licence to the photograph or scan it is noted.

- `tohaku.jpg` - Hasegawa Tohaku, *Pine Trees* (Shorin-zu byobu), left screen, c. 1595. Tokyo National Museum.
- `sesshu_*.jpg` - Sesshu Toyo, *Landscapes of the Four Seasons* (Sansui Chokan, the Long Scroll), 1486. Mori Museum.
- `hyakki_*.jpg` - *Hyakki Yagyo Emaki* (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons), Muromachi period, Shinju-an, Daitoku-ji.

## Board pieces (`pieces/`)

Each figure was cropped from the painting and its paper made transparent.

- `hero.png` - Utagawa Kuniyoshi, *Ishi-jo, wife of Ōboshi Yoshio*, from *Seichū gishin den*, 1848 (the figure alone, cut from its page of text). Public domain.
- `oni.png` - *Oni no nenbutsu* (demon intoning the Buddha's name), Otsu-e folk painting. Public domain.
- `oni3.png` - Kawanabe Kyosai, *Oni in priest's robes*. Public domain.
- `tengu.png` - *Sojobo and Minamoto no Yoshitsune*, hanging scroll. Photograph by Wolfgang Michel, CC BY 3.0.
- `kitsune.png` - *Yako* (field fox), from the scroll *Bakemono no e*, c. 1700. Scan: Brigham Young University, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `yurei.png` - *Yurei*, from *Bakemono no e*, c. 1700. Scan: Brigham Young University, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `tamamo.png` - Katsushika Hokusai, *Nine-Tailed Golden Fox*. Metropolitan Museum of Art. Public domain. Tinted gold.
- `chochin.png` - Toriyama Sekien, *Burabura* (lantern ghost), from *Hyakki Tsurezure Bukuro*, 1784. Public domain.
- `kasa.png` - Kano Enshin, *Kasa-obake* (umbrella yokai), from a *Hyakki Yagyo-zu* scroll, 18th century. Public domain.
- `kappa.png` - Sawaki Sushi, *Kappa*, from *Hyakkai Zukan*, 1737. Public domain.
- `nurikabe.png` - *Nurikabe*, from *Bakemono no e*, c. 1700. Brigham Young University. Public domain.
- `yukionna.png` - *Yuki-onna*, from *Bakemono no e*, c. 1700. Scan: Brigham Young University, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `gasha.png` - Utagawa Kuniyoshi, *Takiyasha the Witch and the Skeleton Spectre*, c. 1844 (detail). Public domain.
- `daruma.png` - Hakuin Ekaku, *Portrait of Daruma*, 18th century. Metropolitan Museum of Art. Public domain.
- `tanuki.png` - Katsushika Hokusai, tanuki in a monk's robe beside a tea kettle (*Bunbuku chagama*). Public domain.
- `shinigami.png` - Katsushika Hokusai, *The Ghost of Kohada Koheiji*, from *One Hundred Ghost Stories*, 1831-32 (detail). Scan: Rawpixel, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `kamaitachi.png` - Ryūkansai Masasumi, *Kamaitachi*, from *Kyōka Hyaku Monogatari*, 1853 (detail: the animal alone, turned head up, its pale fur tinted). Public domain.

## Sound (`audio/`)

- `story.ogg` - *Shika no Tone* (Distant Cry of the Deer), shakuhachi honkyoku played by Araki Kodo III,
  Victor 13029, recorded between 1925 and 1937. Public domain. Restored version from Wikimedia Commons.
- `title.wav` - *Sakura Sakura*, traditional melody (public domain), arranged and synthesised for this game.
- `battle.wav`, `boss.wav` and all sound effects - original, synthesised by `tools/make_audio.py`.
